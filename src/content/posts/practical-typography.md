Matthew Butterick's book is the one I send to every client who asks why their text "feels off". It's free to read online, opinionated in the best way, and written for people who make documents — which, today, is everyone.

The chapter I return to most is the one on [line length](https://practicaltypography.com/line-length.html). His advice — somewhere between 45 and 90 characters per line — is the reason this blog is one narrow column on every screen.

If you only read one part, read the [summary of key rules](https://practicaltypography.com/summary-of-key-rules.html). It fits on one page and fixes most of what goes wrong.
